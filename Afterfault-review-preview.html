THE UNFINISHED PART OF A FINISHED JOB

The repair closes.
The evidence scatters.

A technician’s report in one place. A parts invoice in another. The supplier’s conditions in a document nobody has open.

Potential warranty costs are easy to overlook when every repair needs a fresh search. Afterfault brings the records into one case, references the supplied terms, and shows what still needs attention.

Potential claims only. Eligibility, supplier approval, and payment are never guaranteed.

THE PROPOSED PRODUCTION FOUNDATION
Architecture on
Amazon Web Services
The proposed architecture connects repair evidence, warranty checks, and human review. The interactive demo uses sample data.
01 /
Repair intake
AWS Step Functions
Orchestrate the processing workflow and review states.
02 /
Evidence storage
Amazon S3
Store reports, invoices, equipment evidence, terms, and draft exports.
03 /
Extraction & checks
Amazon Textract · AWS Lambda
Extract document text and fields; run structured date, cost, and required-evidence checks.
04 /
Warranty analysis
Amazon Bedrock
Assist interpretation of supplied wording and draft evidence-linked summaries.
05 /
Human review
Amazon DynamoDB
Store case records, evidence references, and human review decisions.
Extraction reads the document. Rules check the facts. AI assists interpretation. People decide.
